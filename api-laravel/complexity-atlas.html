Systemic patterns

A · ~30 controllers

The permitParams clone

A 48-line, CC-15 parameter-permission method duplicated across every V1 controller — it is the entire ceiling on the risk table. One shared trait or base-method driven by a per-controller field map collapses ~450 complexity points into one tested implementation.

B · ~10 services

The UpdateService::execute ladder

Each ported update service is one 120–190-line method of if (array_key_exists(...)) branches over optional params, porting Rails' params.each blocks. Mechanically compressible to field→assigner maps, but each branch encodes exact Rails update semantics — do not touch while contract waves are landing.

C · 2 files

assignParams twins

Customers\UpsertFromApiService and Customers\UpdateService carry near-identical 80-line param mappers (CC 27/26). Unify behind one mapper after the Customers wave stabilises.

D · leave alone

Faithful validation matrices

SubscriptionCreationValidator (1,233 lines, total CC 181) and Charge::validateAttributes mirror Rails validation rules one-to-one. The complexity is the contract; refactoring these hides diffs against the Rails source.

Read: the risk table is one bug wearing twenty hats. Fix pattern A once, in its own commit, after the current contract wave lands — the risk ceiling drops from 15 to single digits everywhere at once. Pattern B is real but encodes golden-tested semantics; schedule it, don't hero it. Pattern D is the port doing its job.
